# Learn Business Chinese Basic with Phoebe

Six sessions from zero to a first business meeting in Mandarin: the sounds and the four tones, names, titles and business cards, numbers and money, meetings and WeChat, and a first meeting with its follow-up. A real pitch tracker in your browser scores your tones by shape, not height.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-business-chinese-basic-with-phoebe/

| # | Session |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sounds and tones |
| 2 | Names, titles and business cards |
| 3 | The tone trainer |
| 4 | Numbers, money, dates and times |
| 5 | Meetings, calls and WeChat basics |
| 6 | The first meeting, and the follow-up |

The bench in session 3 (`assets/tone-live.js`) runs a YIN pitch tracker on four synthetic voices and five scoring methods. Raw pitch in hertz scores a lower voice 6 of 23; the calibrated, range-scaled method scores every voice 23 of 23; scoring dictionary tones marks five correct tone changes wrong.

Every Chinese term on every page carries its pinyin and English. The running case, Lumen Logistics, is invented.

by Phoebe Fu
