Learn Business Chinese Basic with Phoebe

In Mandarin, 买 (mǎi, buy) and 卖 (mài, sell) differ only in the shape of the pitch, and a buyer who says the second has offered to sell. Six sessions take a complete beginner to a first business meeting in Chinese: the sounds and the four tones, how to address people by title and exchange a card, numbers, money, dates and times, booking and confirming a meeting on WeChat, and the meeting itself with its follow-up. Every Chinese word on every page carries its pinyin and English. A real pitch tracker running in your browser listens to you and scores each tone by its shape against your own voice, so a deep voice and a high voice are judged the same way.

The six sessions 🎯

The running case is Lumen Logistics, an invented Singapore freight company, 鲁门物流 (Lǔmén Wùliú, Lumen Logistics), opening a partnership with a Shanghai distributor. You are the person they send.

🔊 Session 1 · foundational

Sounds and tones

How a syllable is built, pinyin and its tone marks, the four tones as shapes, why 买 (mǎi, buy) and 卖 (mài, sell) must never be confused, and the three tone changes.

▶ Start here 🪪 Session 2 · foundational

Names, titles and business cards

Surname first, address by title, safe defaults when you do not know the title, and how a card is given and received with two hands.

 🎛️ Session 3 · bench night

The tone trainer

A pitch tracker scores four synthetic voices five ways: raw hertz fails a lower voice, calibration rescues a level word, dictionary tones mark correct speech wrong. Then your own voice.

★ the bench 🔢 Session 4 · core

Numbers, money, dates and times

Counting in fours with 万 (wàn, ten thousand), prices, phone numbers, dates in Chinese order and meeting times.

 💬 Session 5 · core

Meetings, calls and WeChat basics

Polite requests, booking and confirming a meeting, and WeChat as the everyday business channel.

 🤝 Session 6 · capstone

The first meeting, and the follow-up

The first five minutes scripted, the numbers said and confirmed, the WeChat follow-up sent the same day. Final scorecard.
