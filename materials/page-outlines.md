## 02-names-titles-and-cards.html · Names, titles and business cards
h1: "Names, titles and <span class="accent">business cards</span>". 🟢 Foundational.
- Part 1 "Surname first": Chinese names are surname then given name; 王 (Wáng, Wang), 李 (Lǐ, Li), 张 (Zhāng, Zhang) as common surnames (say "common", give no ranking figures); you address people by surname + title, never by given name in a first meeting. Figure: a business card drawn front (Chinese) and back (English), arrows mapping surname, given name, title and company between the two sides.
- Part 2 "Titles do the work": 王经理 (Wáng jīnglǐ, Manager Wang), 李总 (Lǐ zǒng, President Li) with the map's caveat that the 总 (zǒng) form is widely heard but no style guide was found, 张老师 (Zhāng lǎoshī, Teacher Zhang, also used for senior experts), 先生 (xiānsheng, Mr) and 女士 (nǚshì, Ms) as safe defaults when the title is unknown. Figure: a decision tree: do you know the title? yes -> surname + title; no -> surname + 先生 (xiānsheng, Mr) / 女士 (nǚshì, Ms).
- Part 3 "Giving and receiving a card": two hands, Chinese side facing the recipient (Columbia LRC, Open University: secondary); read it before putting it away; set it on the table in a meeting. Figure: two figures across a table, a card passed with two hands, the card's orientation drawn.
- Build-along: your self-introduction in five lines: 您好 (nín hǎo, hello, polite); 我姓… (wǒ xìng, my surname is) ; 我叫… (wǒ jiào, my name is); 我是鲁门物流的… (wǒ shì Lǔmén Wùliú de, I am Lumen Logistics' ...); 很高兴认识您 (hěn gāoxìng rènshi nín, pleased to meet you). Embed `<div id="tone-practice"></div>` and load tone-live.js so learners practise 经理 (jīnglǐ, manager) and 公司 (gōngsī, company).
- Quiz: surname order; the safe default title; how to hand over a card. Covered: Columbia LRC and OpenLearn (secondary); 王总 unverified; Lumen constructed. Next: session 3, the tone trainer.

## 04-numbers-money-dates.html · Numbers, money, dates and times
h1: "Numbers, money, <span class="accent">dates</span> and times". 🟡 Core.
- Part 1 "One to ten thousand": 一 to 十 (yī to shí, one to ten), 百 (bǎi, hundred), 千 (qiān, thousand), 万 (wàn, ten thousand); Chinese groups large numbers in fours, so 100,000 is 十万 (shí wàn, ten ten-thousands) and a million is 一百万 (yìbǎi wàn, one hundred ten-thousands). Figure: the same number grouped in threes (English) and in fours (Chinese), with the unit names over each group. Also 两 (liǎng, two of) before measure words and 二 (èr, two) in counting.
- Part 2 "Money and prices": 块 (kuài, yuan, spoken) and 元 (yuán, yuan, written); prices said as number + 块; 十 (shí, ten) versus 四 (sì, four) as the classic misheard pair; 一 (yī, one) said yāo in phone numbers is common practice in mainland speech (state as common practice). Figure: a price tag read aloud, digit by digit, with the tone of each syllable drawn.
- Part 3 "Dates and times": year-month-day order, 星期一 (xīngqī yī, Monday) to 星期日 (xīngqī rì, Sunday), 上午 (shàngwǔ, morning) / 下午 (xiàwǔ, afternoon), 三点 (sān diǎn, three o'clock), 周四下午三点 (zhōusì xiàwǔ sān diǎn, Thursday 3 pm). Figure: a calendar card for Lumen's meeting with each part labelled in order.
- Build-along: read aloud and write: a quantity (2,400 cartons), a price (38,500 yuan), a phone number, the meeting time; check tones with `#tone-practice` (load tone-live.js).
- Quiz: grouping in fours (what is 十万); 两 versus 二; date order. Covered: constructed numbers; no external statistics. Next: session 5.

## 05-meetings-calls-and-wechat.html · Meetings, calls and WeChat basics
h1: "Meetings, calls and <span class="accent">WeChat</span> basics". 🟡 Core.
- Part 1 "Asking politely": 请 (qǐng, please), 麻烦您 (máfan nín, may I trouble you), 可以吗 (kěyǐ ma, is that OK); requests framed as questions; 谢谢 (xièxie, thank you) and 不客气 (bú kèqi, you're welcome). Figure: one request said three ways, from blunt to polite, as a ladder.
- Part 2 "Booking and confirming a meeting": 开会 (kāihuì, have a meeting), 方便吗 (fāngbiàn ma, is it convenient), 我们周四下午三点见 (wǒmen zhōusì xiàwǔ sān diǎn jiàn, see you Thursday at 3 pm); confirming the day before. Figure: a three-message thread drawn as bubbles: propose, confirm, reminder (each message printed with its English beneath).
- Part 3 "WeChat as a work tool": WeChat 微信 (Wēixìn, WeChat) is the default business messaging channel in mainland China (state as widely observed practice, no statistic); voice messages versus text, add contacts by QR code; keep first messages short and addressed by title. Figure: a phone with a WeChat chat, labels for title in the first line, one request, one thanks. Link the Advanced course for register in depth.
- Build-along: write and say a meeting-booking exchange of four messages, each with its English beneath; say the key phrases with `#tone-practice`.
- Quiz: the softest request form; what to confirm the day before; how to open a first WeChat message. Covered: practice described as common, not sourced; Lumen constructed. Next: session 6.
